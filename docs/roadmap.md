# Personal project roadmap

This roadmap favors features that make the tracker enjoyable for one gardener.
Items can move freely as real garden use reveals what matters.

## Now

- Garden journal for watering, feeding, pruning, and observations
- Quick planting lifecycle updates
- Planting filters
- Tasks and harvest history

## Next

- Seed packet and seed inventory tracking
- Reusable seasonal planting plans
- Photos on journal entries
- Pest and disease observation history
- Simple harvest totals by crop and season
- Export the garden journal as CSV

## Later

- Local weather and frost-date guidance
- Recurring tasks
- Companion planting notes
- Garden layout sketch
- Progressive web app and offline capture

## Deliberately postponed

- Multi-tenant administration
- Complex role management
- Infrastructure automation
- Production monitoring and scaling
